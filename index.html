Since 1999, we have been shaping how organizations find, grow, and keep the people who move them forward. One belief has driven that work from the very beginning: That the world needs great talent.

How a leading global player in the industrial sector scaled the growth of its India GCC

The challenge

  • Rapidly build and scale an India Global Capability Center (GCC) across critical engineering, IoT, and software disciplines
  • Overcome low candidate brand awareness compared to pure-play tech conglomerates in competitive Indian metros
  • Address high talent demand, stringent skill calibration, and tight ramp-up timelines

Our solutions

  • Combined integrated employer branding campaigns with aggressive multi-channel talent acquisition pipelines
  • Deployed an agile RPO framework with dedicated recruiter pods, on-site leads, and specialized market mapping
  • Conducted salary benchmarking and rigorous competency assessments to elevate conversion and joining ratios

The impact

  • Scaled headcount from inception to hundreds of specialized engineers within tight delivery milestones
  • Established the multinational giant as a preferred employer brand across major Indian technology hubs
  • Transformed the India center from initial support into a key driver of global engineering innovation

How Blue Yonder scaled fresher hiring in India through a digital-first talent model

The challenge

  • Expand engineering and R&D capability centers across multiple Indian operational hubs (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Coimbatore)
  • Overcome the resource-heavy overhead and slow turnaround of traditional campus hiring models
  • Fairly and accurately evaluate massive volumes of early-career applicants against rigorous technical benchmarks

Our solutions

  • Implemented an end-to-end, digital-first hiring framework powered by automated candidate engagement
  • Integrated standardized online coding and aptitude evaluations to filter applicants objectively at scale
  • Structured high-impact apprentice programs and off-campus recruitment drives supported by streamlined workflow automation

The impact

  • Successfully onboarded hundreds of high-potential early-career engineers across target technology corridors
  • Significantly decreased time-to-hire while eliminating bias through standardized assessment metrics
  • Established a repeatable, scalable model for ongoing workforce expansion in India

How a global consulting leader became an employer of choice for interns in India

The impact

  • Surpassed registration targets by 17% with 11,728 registrations and 124,500+ microsite impressions

  • Achieved a 100% conversion rate with all 65 shortlisted finalists accepting internship offers
